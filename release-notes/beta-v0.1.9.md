Tool windows over the 3D view. Unsigned Linux AppImage and Windows portable exe attached.

What is new since beta-v0.1.8:

- **Floating tool windows.** Every tool panel now opens as a window over the 3D view, in its top-left corner by default. Drag it by its title bar and it remembers where you left it; double-click the title bar to put it back.
- **Boolean and Extrude windows.** One toolbar button each. Pick the parts in labeled slots (Base and Subtract for Difference, Parts for Union and Intersection, Profile for Extrude); whatever is selected fills them first. Extrude has Linear or Rotate, height, twist and centered, or an angle. Nothing is written until Apply.
- **Properties window.** The selected part's parameters, position, gizmo mode, mirror, color, modifier, pattern count and Make variable are all in one window. It folds to its title bar, and that is remembered.
- **One-row toolbar.** The toolbar no longer grows rows when you select something, in sketch mode too. The shading menu moved to the top bar.
- **Sketch grid snap off by default.** Points still snap to corners, midpoints and edges. Your Snap choices are remembered across restarts.
- **No Extrude on 3D parts.** Extrude is greyed out while a 3D part is selected (it used to make the part vanish), and the tooltip says why.
